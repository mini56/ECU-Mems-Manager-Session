# V1.67 — Direct review of 68K command fields

Three ASM/binary examples verified: ADJMP002 (2J), CS73P001 (3 EU3), DS43I003 (3 EU4). In the two MEMS3 examples, 87 and 8A are second-level fields under 3B and 1A; 9A, 9B, 9C and 9E are also second-level fields under 1A. In ADJMP002, 01 is a second-level field under 27. In CS73P001, two 81 comparisons address offsets 0 and 3, not two extra primary services. The 28 binary sites and SHA values are preserved in preuves_directes.json. These observations establish neither semantics nor physical availability and must not be generalized automatically.
