- CI smoke validation may use --reasoning off only for its short deterministic API check
- IA observes already-acquired ECU measurements only and has no ECU command or mutation authority
- legacy iamemstab_clean.cpp, iamemsqualitypatch.cpp and iaresponsecontextpatch.cpp are removed
- packaged uninstaller and install manifest are retained
